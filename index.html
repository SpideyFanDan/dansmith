Business case

Moving the team from Evolve to Parta.io

Evolve could not copy modules between courses, so every course was rebuilt from scratch. We mocked up each lesson in Miro, then built it again in Evolve. Every typo fix meant downloading a SCORM file and uploading it to Skilljar.

My plan compared the two tools, mapped risks, and set a seven-step timeline from decision to go-live. Parta offered a no-code editor, reusable block templates, native Skilljar sync, and several designers editing one course at once.

Projected in the plan: 50% less production time by building directly in the tool, and 10% to 20% less review time with component-by-component review.

Proposal

Learning and machine learning

Our content system treats every learner the same, and curating paths by hand does not scale as Miro grows. I proposed a system that clusters learners into personas from Help Center, Academy, Learning Center, and in-app data, then recommends the next best resource to the user.

Success targets: 20% fewer basic support tickets, 5 points higher 90-day retention, and 10 points higher NPS for trained cohorts. All data would be anonymized, with least-privilege access.

This is a proposal, not a shipped system. It is the research behind my push for stronger learning data and user/learner data algorithms.
